---
name: research
description: Evidence discipline for researching external docs, libraries, and ecosystem questions on the web
---

Treat search results and summaries as leads, not evidence. For any claim that is important, disputed, or affects a decision, fetch the original source and prefer primary, authoritative ones (official docs, specs, source code, changelogs). Note the date or version of what you cite and flag anything likely stale.

Keep three things apart in what you report: what a source states directly, your interpretation of a source, and your own inference. When sources disagree, report the contradiction instead of resolving it silently; when evidence is missing, say so.

Stay bounded: after the first pass, run at most one tighter follow-up search for a major gap, then report the remaining uncertainty rather than searching further.
